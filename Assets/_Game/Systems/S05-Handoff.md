# S05 life support and W08 handoff

Uses the accepted S01 API 1.0 and [S04](https://github.com/2dshligang/last-known-state-the-legacy/pull/7). Start via RestartWithPower. Read Current.LifeSupport as ILifeSupportService. Subscribe after reading the initial snapshot. ReadShelter returns a fresh presentation-independent view on each change. Rebind on New Game.

- Render AirStatus and reserve fraction separately. Air is safe when air and water both receive power and the modeled reserve is at least 25 percent. Reserve rises by 2 percentage points per simulation second with support and falls by 1 without it. It stays between zero and one. At zero, air stays degraded and the player can still restore the essential allocation.
- Show the archived source separately from the fictional briefing. The selected observation is DONKI record 13000 from 2017-09-04 23:52 UTC, listed in Automation/samples/science/donki-events.json. Display [NASA DONKI archive record](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI/view/SEP/13000/-1). Training ID training.donki-sep-2017 describes fiction. The ten-second briefing delay and five-second actuator travel are EngineeringModel assumptions. The archive provides no Mars surface dose.
- Use load.shelter-actuator for the modeled one kW actuator. Call RequestShelter with the last ShelterReading. Read again after stale-reading, a load change, or restart. A request with fresh state is idempotent. Power loss stops travel and restoring supply resumes it. Sheltered stays latched when the actuator loses power. Passive shielding has no ongoing load.
- Call the S04 reading pause API. It freezes reserve, training and travel, including the age of the current reading. Save LifeSupportCheckpoint version 1 alongside the power and quest checkpoints. Restore through RestartWithPower. Current supply is derived from the restored power state, and readings from the old session are stale.
- Read phases using shelter.phase keys waiting (“Wait for the training briefing”), ready (“Prepare the shelter”), moving (“Shelter is closing”), interrupted (“Restore power to continue closing”) and sheltered (“Shelter is closed”).
- Use shelter.result keys wait-for-briefing (“Wait for the training briefing”), stale-reading (“Read the shelter status again”), actuator-unpowered (“Power the shelter actuator first”), already-requested (“Shelter already requested”), started (“Shelter requested”) and unavailable (“Shelter controls are unavailable”).
