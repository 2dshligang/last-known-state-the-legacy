# Scientific integrity

The story takes place in a fictional future. Every scientific statement must be classified during review as one of the following:

1. Established finding — supported by a traceable, real source.
2. Engineering model — a documented assumption or simulation used for gameplay.
3. Fictional story detail — invented for the narrative and not presented as a real discovery.

## Required language

- Organic molecules do not prove the existence of life.
- Potential biosignatures are not confirmed evidence of life.
- Correlation, habitability and biological origin are separate claims.
- Uncertainty and alternative non-biological explanations must remain visible where relevant.

## Sources

Record source title, organization, URL and access date for real scientific claims. Never fabricate NASA citations, instruments, measurements or mission results. If a source cannot be verified, rewrite the content as a clearly fictional or hypothetical statement.
