# Judge route

Use this route to demonstrate one decision at each station. The full New Game campaign is designed for 20–25 minutes. The short presentation uses captioned excerpts of the same route, with traversal and waiting omitted from the presentation. W21 supplies the verified capture and timings. A live run uses the available game controls and completes each objective in order.

## Controls and setup

Use the extracted Windows player. For a full rehearsal, start a New Game on a clean test profile. Follow the English **NEXT** objective at the top of the screen. Use **WASD** or the arrow keys to move, the mouse to look, **E** to interact and **Esc** to pause. Read the opening with **Enter**, or press **Space** to skip it for a short presentation. Panels display their own controls. Many use **Tab** and **Enter** or the mouse. Read captions before advancing. Leave external source links closed during offline play.

## Five moments to show

1. **Restore life support.** Confirm arrival, then open the power console. Select **Air circulation**, **Water supply** and **ECHO archive**. Show the supply preview, choose **Apply allocation**, then **Confirm safe life support**. Explain that starting one optional system can require switching another off.
2. **Read the archive and reconstruct ECHO.** Open **Solar particle observation**, then choose **Mark as read**. Point out its date and the missing Mars surface dose. Open the ECHO replay, demonstrate a model estimate and the missing interval, then choose **Investigate incident**. Collect and order the three recorded clues: old reading received, controller uses the reading, backup power runs out. Choose **Check sequence** and **Confirm ECHO step**. The NASA record provides historical context. The incident logs belong to the story.
3. **Collect samples and run a control.** Follow **NEXT** to the sample locations, record context and collect both rocks and the empty control. At the power console, switch from **ECHO archive** to **Laboratory**, keeping air and water supplied. In the lab run the empty control. The first run finds background. Use **Clean chamber**, run and review a fresh blank, then analyze and review both rocks. Select **Confirm sample analysis**. Show how checking an empty instrument changes what the player can conclude.
4. **Finish the report.** Include fine layers, signs of iron minerals and the passing blank. Choose a cautious sediment interpretation, an alternative such as wind deposition, uncertainty about origin or evidence for life, and a follow-up comparison with a fresh blank. Submit the report, complete its step if prompted and select **Queue accepted report**. Switch the optional laboratory load to **Transmitter** to demonstrate queued delivery while the player moves. Explain that this is simulated local communication.
5. **End at the photograph.** Follow **NEXT** to the overlook and interact with the photograph. Hold the final frame long enough to read **Dad... I did it.** Open the credits after the ending to show the data and asset acknowledgments. For a full verification run, reopen the saved ending through **Continue** and record that it loads.

For the short capture, show each decision together with its visible result. Use an English on-screen caption for any needed explanation, such as “Historical observation: no Mars surface dose” or “Fictional instrument result: check the blank first.” Shelter training and seedling planning can be shown as separate optional excerpts with their own labels. Capture and verify the longer campaign separately, from New Game to the photograph, with ordinary player input.
