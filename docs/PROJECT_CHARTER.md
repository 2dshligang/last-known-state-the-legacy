# Project charter

## Narrative

In 2059, Dmitry leaves Earth with the first ASTERION expedition to investigate ancient Martian environments and potential biosignatures. A cascading technical failure destroys the mission.

In 2089, Dmitry's son Alexei returns to Mars at age 39. He restores the abandoned station, reconstructs archived events and completes his father's unfinished scientific work. The ending shows Alexei looking at his father's photograph, followed by the text:

> Dad... I did it.

## Experience goals

- Deliver a complete 20–25 minute narrative arc.
- Keep exploration focused and readable.
- Integrate science into actions rather than exposition alone.
- Ensure puzzles communicate their state visually and through English text.
- Preserve comprehension with all audio muted.

## Scope guardrails

- One compact Mars station and its immediate scientific work areas.
- A limited power-routing sequence.
- One coherent ECHO reconstruction investigation.
- A focused geological sampling and analysis sequence.
- One authored ending.
